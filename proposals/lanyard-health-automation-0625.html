Automation Consulting Agreement
Between ReRev Labs (Keyona Meeks) and Lanyard Health · Effective June 24, 2025
1. Parties

This Agreement is entered into as of June 24, 2025 between ReRev Labs, operated by Keyona Meeks, Birmingham, AL ("Consultant") and Lanyard Health ("Client").

2. Scope of services

Consultant will perform a Deep Automation Audit & Implementation, which includes:

  • Discovery and mapping of the Client's existing operational workflows
  • Identification and prioritization of automation opportunities
  • Hands-on implementation of agreed-upon automations
  • Documentation of implemented systems and recommendations for future improvements
