A member referral is not a layer.

An existing member bringing a colleague, or a corporate partner introducing us to their sales floor, does not sit in the stack. It runs alongside the whole climb and pulls a qualified target to the front of the line. A warm intro doesn't change who fits, it changes who we reach first.

Read it bottom up: Fit qualifies the pool → Static signals rank receptivity → Momentary signals time the outreach → A member referral jumps the queue

The compass

The climb tells you who and when. The compass tells you where each signal lives and why it sharpens the buyer definition. Expand a group to see its sources. Nothing here is either-or, every source stacks on the others to make the picture more acute.

Where each signal lives
LinkedIn
Promotions, territory changes, and content about leveling up, in real time. The single richest momentary-signal source we have.
Local business journals
"40 under 40" and "women to watch" lists in Birmingham and comparable metros surface exactly the profile we want, pre-vetted by someone else.
Industry association rosters
Women in real estate, women in auto retail, and similar groups are a direct membership list of the standard-fit pool.
Company press releases
DEI launches, ERG announcements, and new leadership hires on the employer side, dated and citable.
Vertical trade press
Auto retail, CRE, and sales enablement trade pubs carry leadership moves and culture stories before they show up anywhere structured.
Podcasts and panels
Women in sales who speak publicly on panels or podcasts are already standing-acuity, they've named the problem out loud.
Conference agendas
Women in sales and similar conferences reveal both attendees worth reaching and companies sponsoring, an employer-lane signal.
Apollo
Named people at fit companies and fit titles, both individual targets and the employer-side economic buyer, with contact fields.
LinkedIn Sales Navigator
Filterable by title, industry, and recent activity, the fastest way to build both lanes' outreach lists at once.
DEI and ERG tracker
A running list of employers in target verticals with active women's programs, the employer-lane target list.
Budget-season calendar
Most professional development and L&D budgets open in Q1 and again pre-fall, a predictable window to time employer outreach against.
Champion language
The exact words members use to describe being left out. It's what makes outreach land, and it exists nowhere you can query.
The breaking point
The specific missed deal or moment that converts a browser into a member. The incident to listen for so we arrive right after it.
Who owns this inside a company
On the employer side, whether it's sales leadership, HR, or DEI who actually holds the budget and the decision.
