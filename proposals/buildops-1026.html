Seven jobs we can automate

BuildOps sets up and runs AI automations for estimating, bidding, subcontractor coordination and project paperwork. Each one below says what changes for your team.

  • Re-pricing a bid after the design changes

    Your estimator reviews a list of what changed and the price impact, instead of re-reading two sets of drawings.

  • Chasing quotes from suppliers and subs

    Requests and reminders go out on schedule. Every reply lands in one sheet you can compare.

  • Matching subs to a bid package

    You get a shortlist of subs for each trade in the package, ready to invite.

  • Writing proposals and capture plans

    You start from a draft built from your past wins and the bid documents, then edit it.

  • Preparing pay applications and compliance paperwork

    A checklist shows which certificates and waivers are in, and which are still missing.

  • Reading long contracts and scope documents

    A one-page summary lists scope, dates, retainage and risk clauses, with the page each came from.

  • Sorting the inbox

    Project email goes to the right person, with a draft reply for the routine ones.

What a better workflow can look like

The job: collecting insurance certificates and lien waivers for a pay application.

Today

A project coordinator emails each sub for their documents. Replies arrive in different formats. Someone checks each one against the contract and chases the missing ones by hand.

With an automation

Requests go out on a schedule. Each reply is filed and checked against a list. The coordinator gets one short list of what is missing and approves each follow-up before it goes out.
