Internal Review // Athlete-Site.com Facebook Campaign

Four Pixel concepts for student athlete parents.

Each concept below targets the same audience, parents whose end goal is a college athletic scholarship, but pulls a different emotional lever. The ask: pick the one most likely to stop a parent mid-scroll and convert them into a lead. Title, hypothesis, and a working demo for each.

NOTE: All numbers in these demos are illustrative placeholders for review only. Production versions use verified recruiting standards and NCAA data.
Concept 01

The Competitiveness Benchmarker

Hypothesis: The single biggest information gap for these parents is not knowing where their kid objectively stands. They judge by local competition, which is misleading. A tool that takes real measurables and returns "your athlete profiles as D2 strong, D1 borderline" delivers an instant, personal verdict.

Why it converts: Both outcomes drive action. Validation makes parents want to capitalize. A wake-up call makes them want help closing the gap. Either way, the natural next step is talking to us.

Where does your athlete really stand?

Demo

Enter your athlete's best mark and see how they profile against typical recruited-athlete ranges by division.

Lead capture moment: "Get the full division-by-division breakdown plus the 3 things that would move your athlete up a tier" → email gate.
Concept 02

The Scholarship Reality Calculator

Hypothesis: Most parents picture a full ride. Outside of headcount sports, almost nobody gets one. Showing the real average award by sport and division, next to what academic merit money could deliver, is a jolt of honesty almost no one else in this space will give them.

Why it converts: The honesty positions us as the trustworthy broker in an industry full of hype. Parents who see the gap between expectation and reality immediately want a strategy.

What is the scholarship actually worth?

Demo

Pick your athlete's sport and add their GPA to compare athletic money against academic merit money.

Lead capture moment: "Download the funding stack worksheet: how families combine athletic, academic, and need-based money" → email gate.
Concept 03

The Recruiting Timeline Checker

Hypothesis: "Are we behind?" is the background anxiety of every recruiting parent. A tool that takes only a grad year and instantly drops a "you are here" pin on the recruiting timeline answers that question in five seconds with zero effort.

Why it converts: Lowest friction of the four concepts, one input. Nearly every parent of a 10th or 11th grader who runs it will see at least one milestone flagged behind schedule, and urgency is the strongest conversion trigger in this audience.

Is your athlete on schedule?

Demo

Select your athlete's graduation year to see what should already be done and what is coming next.

Lead capture moment: "Get the month-by-month recruiting calendar for the class of [year]" → email gate.
Concept 04

The Eligibility Quick Screen

Hypothesis: Parents are terrified that a paperwork or course-selection mistake will quietly disqualify their kid. The NCAA core course and Eligibility Center rules are confusing enough that a 30-second red/yellow/green screen feels like a safety inspection for their child's future.

Why it converts: Fear of irreversible loss is a stronger motivator than aspiration. A single yellow or red flag turns "we should look into this someday" into "we need to fix this now." Narrowest concept of the four, but highest urgency per user.
