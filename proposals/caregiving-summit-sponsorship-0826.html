The Caregiving Through the Lens of Love, Health, and Wealth Summit brings family caregivers together with the people who shape their options — employers, health systems, financial institutions, and community organizations. Caregiving sits at the intersection of three pressures that are almost never addressed in the same room: the emotional weight of care, the health of the person providing it, and the financial exposure created when a family is unprepared. Sponsorship places your brand in front of an audience actively making decisions about care, work, and money, and signals a commitment to a population Birmingham institutions are only beginning to serve.
