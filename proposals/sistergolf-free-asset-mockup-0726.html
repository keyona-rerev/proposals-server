The moment she needed this

Rachel spent ten months building a relationship with a lead orthopedic surgeon at a large Atlanta hospital system, targeting a $1.8M implant contract renewal. She had the clinical case dialed in, the pricing, the follow-through. She was doing everything her playbook said to do.

She lost the account to a male competitor rep. She found out later, from a mutual contact, that the rep had played in the hospital foundation's annual charity golf classic with the same surgeon two months before the decision. Not as a formal pitch, just the four hours of course time that made him feel like a known quantity.

"I'd been invited to that same tournament. I didn't go because I didn't know what I was doing. That's the deal I lost."

What happens next: Rachel scrolls Facebook two weeks later and sees the SisterGolf "Everyone Has a Coach" ad. She taps it. Here's what she experiences.
