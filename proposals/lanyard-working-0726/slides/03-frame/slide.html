The frame

Two priorities,
one system.

You named LinkedIn automation and an email nurture sequence as two jobs. They're not. They're the two engines of a single nurture system — one running outside the funnel, one inside it.

1

LinkedIn — passive engine

Runs out of the funnel. Content and trust signals that keep you warm in front of people before they're ever a live deal. Set-and-forget by design.

2

Email — active engine

Runs inside the funnel. High-touch, deliberate, pushing a live prospect toward a clear yes or a clean no. This is where timing matters.
