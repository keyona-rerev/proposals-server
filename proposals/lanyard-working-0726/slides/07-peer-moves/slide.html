Nurture like peers

These are peers,
not leads to hand-hold.

Straight from your sales coach, and it's the spine of the whole approach. You're not chasing — you're holding a mutual agreement. Two moves do the heavy lifting.

1

Flip the follow-up burden

At the close, ask: "When should I expect to hear from you by?" Now they set the date. Your follow-up is never nagging — it's honoring their own timeline.

2

The day-after check-in

One full business day past the date you agreed: "Hope everything's okay — today's when we'd said there'd be word on this, so just checking in." Warm, peer-to-peer, and it quietly reasserts the agreement.
