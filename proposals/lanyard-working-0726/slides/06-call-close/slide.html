The one dependency

The whole in-funnel system
rests on how you close the call.

Automated follow-up has nothing to fire against unless every sales call ends with a concrete next step. This is the load-bearing condition — get it right and the rest runs itself.

The rule: no call ends open. Every call closes with either the next call already on the calendar, or a concrete deadline with a named owner and a specific deliverable.

Good close

"Great — I'll send the risk summary by Thursday, and we'll reconnect on the 12th at 10am." Booked. Dated. Owned.

Open close

"Sounds good, I'll follow up soon." Nothing to nurture against. This is the one to eliminate.
