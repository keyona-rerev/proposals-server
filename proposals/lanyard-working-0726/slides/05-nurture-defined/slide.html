Nurture, defined

Two kinds of nurture.

Before we automate anything, we name the two modes — because they behave completely differently and use different tools.

Outside the funnel
  • Passive & broad. You're staying visible, not chasing.
  • Content, thought leadership, trust signals — speaking at a conference, showing up in the feed.
  • This is what the LinkedIn engine handles on autopilot.
Inside the funnel
  • Active & high-touch. A little hand-holding is fine here — you're going for the decision.
  • You're driving toward a yes or a no, not just staying warm.
  • This is the email sequence — and it needs a playbook, not just software.
