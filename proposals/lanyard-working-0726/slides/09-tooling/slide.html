Tooling call

Playbook before software.

The instinct is to reach for a CRM. We're not doing that — not because the tech is hard, but because the sequence isn't proven by hand yet.

No HubSpot. It does far too much for what you need, and I won't rebuild a custom CRM you'd have to move onto. Wrong weight for the job.
Validate the cadence manually first. Run the two moves by hand for a short stretch. Automating a rhythm you haven't run yet is backwards.
Then a light Google Apps Script helper. Once it's proven, a small tool drafts the right message at the right trigger — high-touch without the mental overhead. Just enough, nothing more.
