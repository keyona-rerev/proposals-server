Why this system exists

that cause paralysis.

The conversion artifact. Like Prism's enterprise process — a tailored piece that advances the sale. For Lanyard, e.g. a short anticipated risk profile (say, beneficiaries leaving an institution). One repeatable, brandable template, so "supportive material" is a real deliverable, not a worry.
