This Fall

Learn to play golf with confidence

Ready to finally learn the game, not just how to swing a club?

This fall, the SisterGolf Signature Experience returns with a complete learning journey designed for women who are brand new to golf or still don't feel comfortable stepping onto the course.

From your first putt to confidently walking nine holes, you'll learn the skills, rules, etiquette, strategy, and golf language you need to feel prepared to actually play the game.

More than lessons: a complete golf experience

Your Fall Cohort includes 4 Virtual Education Sessions and 5 In-Person Group Lessons, covering putting, chipping, pitching, full swing and driver through to scoring, handicap, pace of play, course management, and preparing for tournaments and corporate outings.

You'll also receive the SisterGolf Course Guide, access to the SisterGolf community, and opportunities to keep growing through optional Practice Sessions and Play Dates.

By the end, the goal is simple: feel ready to step onto the course and play nine holes with confidence.
