One level up

Loops

An agent repeating cycles of work until a stop condition is met. Four kinds, by what you hand off.

LoopYou hand offUse it when
Turn-basedThe checkYou're exploring or deciding. Every prompt you send is already one of these.
Goal-basedThe stop conditionYou know what done looks like and can measure it.
Time-basedThe triggerThe work recurs on a schedule, or watches something outside your project.
ProactiveThe prompt itselfThe work is recurring, well-defined, and you're not in the room.
The one to learn first

Goal loops

One turn often isn't enough. A goal loop keeps working until your condition is met — not until it decides it's good enough.

/goal get every student record in the sheet to have a complete intake date, stop after 5 tries
  • 1
    Define done. Deterministic beats vague — a count, a threshold, a passing check.
  • 2
    Cap the turns. "Stop after five tries" is the whole safety mechanism.
  • 3
    Let it check itself. The clearer the criteria, the less you referee.
🎯

If you can't say what done looks like, you don't want a loop yet. You want a conversation.
