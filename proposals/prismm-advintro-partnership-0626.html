Prismm is opening a single distribution partnership in the wealth management and financial services space — and we want to build it with Advintro.

The opportunity

Community banks and credit unions are managing the largest intergenerational wealth transfer in American history without the infrastructure to handle it. Prismm is the inheritance platform built specifically for that gap — helping institutions retain assets, deepen member relationships, and turn the estate transition moment into a long-term trust event.

The sales motion is relationship-heavy and senior-executive driven. That's where Advintro has spent decades. This is a natural fit — and we want Advintro to be the firm that gets there first.

The proposed terms
Founding Partner Structure
60%
Revenue share on the first three closed deals
Advintro earns 60% of net contract value on each of the first three institution-level deals sourced and closed through the partnership. Paid at contract execution — not on a timeline.
3
Deal threshold, then we renegotiate
After the first three closings, both parties enter a formal renegotiation based on real deal velocity and terms that reflect the full partnership value.
0
Upfront retainer or monthly fee
No cash commitment from Prismm until a deal closes. Advintro's risk is covered by the outsized share — not papered over with a monthly draw.

Why 60%? Here's the honest framing.

Prismm is mid-raise. We are closing a round, and we are building distribution in parallel — because the institutions Advintro has access to are exactly the proof points that make that round compelling. We're not asking Advintro to take a leap on faith. We're offering an economics structure that reflects that we're asking for their network before we can offer standard cash terms.

The 60% figure exists because it's the number that makes this real for Advintro — not a symbolic gesture or a "we'll circle back." When a deal closes, Advintro gets paid at a rate that reflects founder-level risk and relationship-level contribution. That's the trade.

What Advintro is working with

If the first three deals close the way we believe they will, Advintro will have helped define how a category-creating product found its market — and they'll have the economics to show for it.
