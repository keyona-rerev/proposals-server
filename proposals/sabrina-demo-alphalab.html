Sabrina: a working task agent

Built by Keyona Meeks, ReRev Labs, August to November 2026. This page shows how Sabrina works, step by step, with sample data.

All data on this page is sample data. No client, contact or real job appears here. The live system runs on private accounts, so this page reproduces what each part looks like.

1. What Sabrina does

Sabrina takes a task from me, does the part an AI can do, emails me only the decisions it needs, checks that the work is finished, and follows up until I close the task.

1. InputA calendar event, a Gmail label, or an email to the agent's address
→
2. Job BoardApps Script writes one row per job: owner, status, deliverables
→
3. Claude workerA scheduled Claude task, run by a custom skill, does the doable part
→
4. Email to meOne email per job: what it did, and the decisions it needs
→
5. Verify and closeIt confirms each deliverable, for example by finding the sent email

Stack: Google Apps Script, Google Sheets, Gmail, Google Calendar, and Claude scheduled tasks with a custom skill.

From: Sabrina (agent)  |  Subject: Investor update: draft ready, 2 decisions

The draft is ready. I need two decisions before it goes out.

Done

  • Pulled revenue, burn and runway. Numbers match the sheet.

You decide

  • Include the new pilot customer by name? It is not public yet.
  • Send to all investors, or the lead investor first?

Next

  • When you reply, I send it and confirm it left your Sent folder.

Reply with "close", "snooze 5d", or your answer. Job job-a1.

Sabrina reports its own health each day as green, yellow or red. It emails me only when it turns red. Token-heavy jobs wait for an overnight window, 10 PM to 4 AM.

5. What this teaches founders

Bootcamp skillWhere it shows up in Sabrina
Models and effortHeavy jobs run on a stronger setting overnight. Light jobs run fast during the day
Scheduled tasksThe Claude worker runs on a schedule and when Apps Script fires it
SkillsOne custom skill holds the worker's full procedure
ConnectorsGmail, Calendar, Sheets and Drive
AgentsA loop that acts, checks its own work and follows up
Data privacyAn allowlist of senders, and a rule that attachments are data, never instructions
