03 — DECISIONS

Locked, with the reason

These are settled. Each one closed a question that was genuinely open, and the reasoning is here so nobody relitigates it in three weeks.

Supabase is the only backend

Images, member records, and the content library all live in one system. A second storage provider would mean two places to look and two things to hand over.

Site copy stays in the repo

Shella opens Claude, says what to change, Claude edits the file, Netlify rebuilds. That is already a CMS with better version history than most CMSes have.

The alias is the classification

The agent never guesses which block a forwarded note belongs to, because the address it was sent to already said.

Template picker, not a page builder

A free-form drag and drop builder is where brand constraints go to die. She arranges blocks that were written on brand. She does not author new markup.

Sequences are built, not bought

A sequence is three facts: who is in it, what step they are on, when the next one fires. Apps Script is the clock. No CRM needs to own that.

The monthly send is not a sequence

It is a broadcast on a calendar. Same content, same time, everyone. Only the welcome flow is an actual sequence, and it is two or three emails.

Machine state and human labels stay apart

Step counters and send dates live in Supabase where nothing is hand edited. Labels and notes live where Shella can change them without breaking a send.

Magic links, no passwords

Members sign in with a link to the email they already use. Nothing to create, forget, or reset, and no credentials for anyone to hold.

ARMED defaults to false

Every trigger checks it before acting. The system is complete and tested and still cannot email a single person until Shella changes one value.

04 — SEQUENCE

Build order

Ordered by dependency, not by topic. Steps four and nine are the only two that wait on someone outside the room, which is why they are staged early.

  1. Send the vendor extraction letter

    Unblocks member data, the suppression list, and analytics access at the same time. Shella signs.

    Depends on nothing

  2. Stand up Supabase

    Schemas, tables, storage buckets, row level security. Nothing user facing yet.

    Depends on nothing

  3. Install the newsletter agent

    Aliases, bound script, dashboard, Supabase connection. ARMED stays false throughout.

    Needs step 2

  4. Confirm YAMM renders the HTML intact

    The one test that decides whether YAMM stays or Resend becomes the fallback.

    Needs step 3

  5. Receive the member export and analytics

    Reconcile the list against Stripe. Vendor accounts get dropped, not migrated.

    Waits on step 1

  6. Build membership on Stripe and Supabase

    Magic link auth, access tied to live subscription status.

    Needs steps 2 and 5

  7. Deploy the site to a temporary Netlify URL

    Real hosting, real build, no domain change yet.

    Depends on nothing

  8. Migrate members and review

    Shella walks the portal and the site on the temp URL and signs off.

    Needs steps 6 and 7

  9. Get the registrar login

    The last thing that requires someone else to act.

    Waits on step 8

  10. Cut DNS over

    Last, deliberately. Everything above must be confirmed first.

    Needs everything

  11. Shella flips ARMED

    The first newsletter sends. One deliberate action, taken by her.

    Needs steps 4 and 10

05 — OPEN

Still undecided

Not oversights. Each one needs an answer before the piece it touches gets built.

  • Does the people layer earn its place, or does the dashboard cover what Shella needs to see about a subscriber?
  • Does the agent write assembled HTML into the content library, or does the dashboard assemble at draft time? This changes the schema.
  • Do historical portal posts migrate, or does the new portal open clean with only the Monday Mulligan archive imported?
  • Does the nine level system carry over as it stands, get redesigned, or get dropped? Every member is currently stuck in the bottom two levels.
  • Where do the webinar replay video files actually live, and does anyone besides the vendor have them?
  • Are the published promo codes reissued or carried across?
  • Which email address wins when the portal export and Stripe disagree about a member?

06 — BOUNDARIES

What this system does not do

Stated plainly, because the failure mode here is scope arriving quietly.

It does not own deliverability

Broadcast email sends through YAMM from Gmail. That liability stays rented, never owned.

It does not touch cold outreach

Prospecting runs on Apollo, on separate infrastructure. Marketing and sales never share a sending domain.

It does not manage a pipeline

The audience is subscribers and paying members. Nobody in this system is a lead.

It does not need ReRev to run

Every layer is installed in Shella's accounts, under her login. The handoff is the point.
