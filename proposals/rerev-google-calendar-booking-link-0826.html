Make a booking link with Google Calendar

If you have Google Workspace, you already have a scheduling tool. Here is how to set up a bookable link in a couple of minutes, without paying for a separate one.

Requires a Google Workspace account. Appointment schedules are not available on free personal Gmail accounts.

1 Open Booking pages

In Google Calendar, look at the left sidebar under the mini calendar. Find Booking pages and click the + next to it.

2 Set the duration and your hours

A panel opens on the left. Fill in three things:

  • Title. This is what people see when they book, so name it for them, not for you. Discovery Call works better than Intro.
  • Appointment duration. How long each booking runs.
  • General availability. The days and hours you are bookable. Set a day to unavailable with the circle icon, add a second block of hours with the plus, or copy one day's hours to the rest with the copy icon.

Click Next when those are set.

3 Finish and copy the link

The next screen covers the details around the booking: which calendar it checks, how much notice you need before someone can book, how far ahead they can book, buffer time between appointments, and what you want to ask them when they book.

Save when you are done. Open the schedule again and use the share option to copy the booking link. That link is what goes in your email signature, on your site, and anywhere you currently paste a scheduling URL.

Worth doing while you are in there: the questions you add to the booking form are the only structured information you get about someone before you meet them. Ask for the two or three things that actually change how you run the call, and nothing else. Every extra field is another reason for someone to abandon the booking.

Published by ReRev Labs. Google occasionally changes this interface, so if a label has moved, the sequence is still the same: sidebar, then duration and hours, then booking details.
