Why it is ordered this way

Needs you sits above the numbers on purpose. A digest that opens with counts trains her to skim it. A digest that opens with three named people who are waiting on a reply gets read every time, and the counts are still there underneath for whenever she wants them.

The subject line is generated, not fixed. It leads with whatever is most urgent that week, so the inbox preview alone tells her whether to open it. If nothing needs her it becomes something like Lanyard leads: quiet week, 7 in nurture.

The pattern for every client install

Every system update email is HTML.

Easier to scan, and it lets the urgent block actually look urgent.

Every one links back to its dashboard.

Twice. A button near the top and a text link in the footer. The client never has to remember or bookmark anything, because the email they already get is the front door.

Action before information.
