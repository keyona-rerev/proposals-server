Investment Committee: Role & Decision Framework

As members of the BlackTech Capital Investment Committee, your responsibility is to rigorously evaluate investment opportunities against two core questions:

🎯 Question 1: Thesis Alignment

Does this opportunity align with BTC's promised investment thesis?

CleanTech/ClimateTech solution + Underrepresented founder + Venture-scale market potential

💪 Question 2: Investment Quality

Is this a solid and respectable investment opportunity?

Strong fundamentals, defensible market position, credible execution path, founder quality

Your IC Responsibilities

  • Evaluate consistency with Fund thesis, stage, geography, and risk profile
  • Provide sectoral, technical, and operational insight
  • Challenge assumptions and identify potential risks
  • Conduct additional research or diligence as requested
  • Contribute informed recommendations: INVEST / PASS / FURTHER DILIGENCE

Note: The IC recommendation is advisory. Final investment decision authority rests exclusively with the General Partners, pursuant to the Fund's Limited Partnership Agreement.

Evaluation Criteria & Framework

Each opportunity is evaluated on the following weighted criteria to ensure rigorous, consistent analysis:

Criterion Definition Weight
Market Opportunity Venture-scale TAM, growth trajectory, customer demand signals, regulatory tailwinds 25%
Business Model Scalability Revenue generation potential, unit economics, path to profitability, capital efficiency 20%
Go-To-Market Validation Customer traction, channel proof, acquisition metrics, market acceptance 20%
Competitive Moats & IP Proprietary technology, defensible position, team expertise, first-mover advantage 10%
Founder & Team Quality Domain expertise, execution track record, team composition, fundraising savvy 25%

Decision Scale

75–100: INVEST

Strong alignment to thesis, excellent fundamentals, clear path to impact and returns.

50–74: FURTHER DILIGENCE

Promising but needs validation on specific criteria before final decision.

0–49: PASS

Does not meet Fund requirements or criteria at this time.

Kiwi Charge
UNDER IC REVIEW: First-Time Investment
Autonomous EV Charging | Robotics + AI

Executive Summary

Founders & Team
4 Active Co-Founders

Abdel Ali, Harshit Madaan, Aamir Abubakkar, and Jumana Fathima. All-BIPOC founding team. 3 full-time and 2 part-time team members plus 5 interns. HQ at the Clean Energy Zone at Toronto Metropolitan University; also part of YSpace at York University. Federally incorporated June 2023.

Stage & Raise
Seed | Seeking $2M

$600K in grants and $900K in investment to date. Round funds product development, operations, marketing, and IP.

Thesis Alignment: ✓ EV Infrastructure + Robotics ✓ All-BIPOC Founding Team ✓ Venture Scale ($112B TAM)

Problem & Solution

Problem: 1 in 3 North Americans live in multi-tenant buildings and often cannot charge at home. Fixed chargers are expensive, slow to install, require electrical upgrades, trenching and permitting, and suffer from low utilization.

Solution: Instead of wiring a charger to every parking stall, Kiwi Charge brings the charge to the car. Autonomous, AI-driven EV charging robots navigate to parked EVs and charge multiple vehicles without human intervention. Each building starts with one robot and adds a second once the building exceeds 30 EV owners, supporting up to 60 EV customers per building.

Technology & Cost Advantage

Unit Economics vs. Fixed Chargers
A single Kiwi robot services the equivalent of roughly 30 Level 2 charging spaces at about 40% of the cost of conventional installation, with 3x utilization and 60% lower installation costs.
AI Layer
AI-based load management, smart scheduling, building dashboards, and personalized charging based on user driving and charging habits.
Business Model
Hardware sales plus recurring subscription revenue: Kiwi Dock units, Kiwi Charge robots, and monthly EV-user subscriptions at $149/month.

Carbon Utilization | Carbon-Negative Industrial Materials

Executive Summary

Founder
Black Gay Male Founder

Carbon utilization company transforming captured CO₂ into high-value industrial materials.

Capital Raised
~$6.3M to date

Including $4.3M in non-dilutive funding from Breakthrough Energy.

Thesis Alignment: ✓ Carbon Utilization ✓ Underrepresented Founder ✓ Venture Scale ($40B TAM)

Problem & Solution

Problem: Acrylonitrile is a critical industrial chemical used in water purification polymers, carbon fiber, plastics, textiles, mining, pulp and paper, personal care, and infrastructure. The industry has relied on the same fossil-based petrochemical production process for over 50 years.

Solution: Mars produces carbon-negative acrylonitrile from captured CO₂, water, electricity, and ammonia as a physically verifiable, drop-in alternative for existing industrial supply chains, turning everyday materials into long-term carbon storage products.

Validation & Moats

SNF Validation
Mars' acrylonitrile was validated by SNF, a global leader in water-soluble polymers, and met performance standards across SNF's product portfolio.
Industry First
Mars' product became a physically verifiable drop-in alternative in an industry that historically lacked viable low-carbon acrylonitrile substitutes. Carbon negative, no incumbent toxic by-products, and potential to improve water production by 40%.
Breakthrough Energy Backing
$4.3M in non-dilutive funding from Breakthrough Energy, plus advancing supplier validation, commercial demonstration plant design, offtake development, and strategic partnerships with multi-billion-dollar industrial customers.
