The partner channel is not a layer.

Jack Henry does not sit in the stack. It is a core relationship that reads as legitimacy to a bank or credit union, and it runs alongside the whole climb to pull a qualified target to the front of the line. A warm intro does not change who fits, it changes who we reach first.

The compass

The climb tells you who and when. The compass tells you where each signal lives and why it sharpens the buyer definition. Expand a group to see its sources. Nothing here is either-or, every source stacks on the others to make the picture more acute.

Where each signal lives
CFPB complaints open
Deceased-account and estate complaints are dated, public admissions of the exact pain. Density ranks institutions by felt pain, the sharpest acute signal you can pull without a conversation.
NCUA and FDIC orders open
A consent order touching deposit operations or dormant accounts means a remediation mandate with budget already attached. That is a live trigger, not a maybe.
Merger approvals
A pending or approved merger means a core migration and a deposit-book merge, which is the moment deceased-account backlogs get exhumed in diligence.
Call reports
NCUA and FFIEC filings give the deposit base, the member-age proxy, and the net-worth trajectory. The raw inputs for the exposure score and for fit itself.
Census age data
Where depositors skew old, which is the demographic spine of standard fit and of who hits the death wave first and hardest.
LinkedIn
A new CEO, COO, or experience leader, in real time. That is the mandate window, a six-to-twelve-month opening where someone is buying things to make a mark.
Banking trade press
American Banker and The Financial Brand carry mergers, leadership moves, and strategy, often before a trigger shows up in anything structured.
Podcasts and agendas
Conference agendas, podcast appearances, and shareholder letters reveal who is publicly vocal on deposit growth and wealth transfer. That is the standing-acuity signal that they already get it.
Apollo
The named people at a fit institution, both the economic buyer and the likely champion, with contact fields. The bridge from a target list to an actual outreach list.
Core-vendor lists
Who runs Silverlake or Symitar, which is both a legitimacy and warm-path signal and the seed of the peer cluster you can sequence through.
Exposure score
Depositor-age proxy times digital-maturity gap. Turns fit from a yes or no into a ranking, so you can sort the whole universe by who sits on the wave first.
Core-contract clock
Core contracts run five to seven years. Year five or six is a re-evaluation window you can predict a year ahead and arrive early on.
Seasonal calendar
Deaths peak in winter and the escheatment deadline is fixed, so felt pain and compliance pressure spike on a calendar you can time outreach against.
Orphaned-owner map
Who actually owns a customer death inside the institution. Usually nobody cleanly, which is exactly why the pain has a champion but no fix, and the vacuum becomes the wedge.
Champion language
The precise words your champion uses for this problem. It is what makes outreach land, and it exists nowhere you can query.
The breaking point
The specific failure that converts a non-buyer, a family escalation, a lawsuit, a missed escheat deadline. The incident to listen for so you arrive right after it.
