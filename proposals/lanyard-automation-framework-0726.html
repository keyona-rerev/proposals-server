Symptom Awareness
Help problem-unaware prospects recognize the warning signs and hidden costs of the status quo.
Lanyard example
"Signs your credentialing process is costing you more than you think"
Targets practice managers who don't yet know they have a credentialing problem. Surfaces symptoms: delayed enrollments, revenue gaps, manual errors, provider frustration.
Format
Blog article LinkedIn post
Current state
Weak — 62/100
One blog post touches symptoms loosely. No piece is built specifically around this job.
02
Solution Education
Help problem-aware prospects understand their options, how to evaluate them, and where the alternatives fall short.
Lanyard example
"In-house vs. outsourced vs. platform: how to evaluate your credentialing options in 2025"
Teaches the evaluation framework before Lanyard is in the running. Builds trust by being genuinely useful to someone who might not choose Lanyard.
Format
Comparative guide Checklist
Current state
Gap — 30/100
No evaluation framework or comparative content exists. Biggest single content gap after proof.
03
Value Proposition
Make the case for why Lanyard specifically — not just why help in general.
Lanyard example
"How Lanyard cuts credentialing errors to zero — and what that means for your revenue"
Homepage already carries strong numbers: 0% error rate vs 30-40% industry standard, 50% faster prep time. Needs a dedicated page that argues directly against the alternatives by name.
Format
Explainer page One-pager
Current state
Partial — 58/100
Homepage carries this job. No dedicated page and no named competitor comparison yet.
04
Customer Stories
Let someone else say it worked, with a number attached.
Lanyard example
"How [Practice Name] got 4 providers credentialed in 6 weeks without hiring a credentialing coordinator"
Named practice, specific timeline, specific outcome, specific alternative avoided. Aggregate stats ("300+ enrollments") do not count. One named story outperforms any number of anonymous claims.
Format
Case study Customer interview
Current state
Gap — 8/100
Single biggest gap. No named customer, no testimonial, no attributed before-and-after anywhere on the site.
05
Product Deep Dive
Help someone who is already sold picture using Lanyard on day one.
Lanyard example
"What onboarding a new NP through three payers actually looks like inside Lanyard"
Walks through a specific use case end to end: what the founder enters, what happens automatically, what the status view looks like, how follow-ups are handled. Removes the "but what does this actually do" anxiety from a near-close prospect.
Format
Demo video Workflow walkthrough
Current state
Thin — 44/100
/enrollments page exists but is too thin. No demo video, no walked-through workflow, no specific use case.
Priority order for production: Customer Stories first (highest leverage, lowest current score, requires Kay's input to source a willing customer). Solution Education second (no-cost, no-customer required, builds trust with everyone in the funnel). Value Proposition third (dedicated page, already have the numbers). Product Deep Dive fourth (video or walkthrough). Symptom Awareness last (already partially addressed).
