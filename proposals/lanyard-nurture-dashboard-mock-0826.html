Things this mock is asking you to decide

Does Kay edit copy here, or in a doc?

A textarea is fine for tweaks but bad for real writing. The alternative is each step pointing at a Google Doc she drafts in, and the dashboard just shows which doc.

Can she add and remove steps, or only edit existing ones?

Adding steps is easy to build and easy to misuse. A four step sequence quietly becoming nine is how nurture turns into spam.

Does changing a sequence affect people already in it?

Right now it does, from their next step onward. The other option is that changes only apply to new entrants, which is safer and more confusing.

Should she be able to write a one off email from here?

Would stop her leaving the dashboard to do the thing she actually wants, but it starts turning this into a CRM.

Who else sees this?

The VA and the CS hire. If they get access, the pipeline table needs an owner column and the settings tab probably needs locking.

What happens at the end of a sequence?

Nobody has decided. Options are silently done, back to a list for manual follow up, or a task on her calendar.
