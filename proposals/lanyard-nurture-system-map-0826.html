Who picks the archetype

Blocks: nothing downstream. This is the architecture call of the session.

Model reads the transcript

Classification runs on the call text.

Consequence: Fully hands off, but it is only as good as the archetype definitions, which have not been tested live yet. A wrong tag sends the wrong sequence to a real prospect.

Kay tags it herself

Consequence: Ships today, no transcript dependency, and every tag becomes training data. Costs her about thirty seconds per call. Swap in the model later without touching anything downstream.
Ask her

  • How wrong would a mistagged prospect feel to you?
  • How many discovery calls are you actually running per week right now?

Whose mailbox sends

Blocks: nothing today, but changing it later means rewriting the copy and the sender config.

On Aug 3 she said she is bringing on an offshore VA and a customer success specialist.

Kay personally

Sends from kay@lanyardhealth.com, first person.

Consequence: Highest reply rate at this stage. Does not survive delegation. Handing it to a VA means rewriting every email.

Lanyard shared voice

Written as the company, delegated send.

Consequence: A VA can run it on day one. Slightly cooler tone. Needs a Workspace group or delegation set up before the copy is written, not after.
Ask her
  • What is the actual start date on the VA and the CS hire?
  • Will they answer replies, or only send?
  • Do you want prospects to think they are talking to you, or to Lanyard?
