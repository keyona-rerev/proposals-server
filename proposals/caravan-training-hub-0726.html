The Standard — Scoping Call Follow-Up

When it starts

Once notes or a transcript from the call exist.

What it needs

The call notes/transcript, and past client work examples to think alongside.

How you do it

  1. Read the transcript/notes.
  2. Have a thinking conversation about this prospect — questions that pull the insight out of you (what's unique here, what it reminds you of), using the transcript and past work together. Not a contrarian pushing back — a partner pulling out what you already know.
  3. From that thinking, write the recap and pick the intros, pitch, and materials that actually match.
  4. Send within 48 hours.

What good looks like

  • Sent within 48 hours of the call.
  • At least one specific past client example included, matched to their situation.
  • Named intro(s) offered where relevant — not generic.
  • Every claim in the recap traces back to something actually said in the transcript — nothing invented or assumed.

The one step worth reusing

The thinking conversation itself — pulling out what's unique about this prospect using the transcript and past work together. This is the first skill built in Mission 6, not a recap generator.

You now have a written spec for your own job — the seed for the tools (Mission 5), the skill (Mission 6), and the eval (Mission 7).
